The FRIS workflow

Turn a project area into a ranked verification queue.

FRIS combines multiple Earth-observation signals and delivers results in formats that can be reviewed by GIS and field teams.

01 / SUBMIT

Provide an AOI

Start with a project boundary and the required analysis date.

02 / ANALYSE

Measure change

Review vegetation, moisture, burn and surface-change signals.

03 / CORROBORATE

Check persistence

Use radar and multi-date evidence to reduce isolated false alarms.

04 / DELIVER

Prioritise the field

Receive ranked locations, evidence layers, maps and reports.

Method and capability

Multiple signals. One practical decision workflow.

Optical change

Sentinel-2 time series support NDVI, NDMI, NBR and BSI-based vegetation, moisture and disturbance screening.

Radar corroboration

Sentinel-1 VH and VV backscatter provide an additional signal when optical imagery is affected by cloud or seasonal variation.

Persistence and patches

Repeated evidence and connected-patch analysis help rank locations that deserve attention before field verification.

Context layers

WorldCover, Hansen forest history, GEDI, terrain and climate context help describe the conditions around each result.

Evidence and validation

Results designed to be reviewed, not overstated.

FRIS keeps data gaps, review cases and uncertainty visible so that results can support responsible field decisions.

Responsible use

FRIS outputs are screening evidence for monitoring and field verification. They are not legal proof, ground truth, illegal-activity determinations, certified carbon verification or regulatory certification.
